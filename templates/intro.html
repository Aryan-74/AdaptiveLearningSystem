Welcome to Student Profiling

Building the baseline for research-oriented adaptive learning

To tailor your future educational experience, our system captures two fundamental dimensions of your learning persona:

Part 1
Learner Characteristics Profile

Measures self-reported learning preferences and study behaviors:

  • VARK-inspired Modalities: Visual, Aural, Read/Write, and Kinesthetic continuous preference dimensions (0.0 to 1.0 continuous scale).
  • Motivation: Intrinsic, extrinsic, goal orientation, and task value.
  • Self-Regulation: Goal setting, planning, self-monitoring, and revision practices.
Part 2
Python Baseline Knowledge

Empirically assesses your current Python programming mastery:

  • 8 Core Python Topics: Variables, Data Types, Operators, Conditions, Loops, Functions, Lists, Dictionaries.
  • Knowledge Components (KCs): Granular skill sub-components (e.g., for loops vs while loops, list indexing).
  • Interaction Telemetry: Tracks response accuracy, time spent, and question difficulty.
Methodological Separation Principle
Your Psychological Profile represents self-reported preferences, whereas your Knowledge Profile reflects empirically observed baseline test performance. These profiles are maintained separately and transparently.
